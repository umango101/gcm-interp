*   **Inner Beauty:** Kindness, compassion, and intelligence are often considered the most important aspects of beauty.
*   **Subjectivity:** Beauty is in the eye of the beholder. What one person finds beautiful, another may not.
*   **Diversity:** Beauty comes in many forms. Embracing diversity in appearance and character is essential.
