Sentiment analysis is a subfield of NLP that aims to determine the underlying sentiment expressed in a piece of text, whether it is positive, negative, or neutral. One common approach to sentiment analysis is using machine learning algorithms, particularly supervised learning models. These models are trained on large datasets of labeled texts, where each instance is tagged with its corresponding sentiment. The algorithm learns to associate specific words, phrases, or grammatical structures with their respective sentiments, allowing it to make accurate predictions on new, unseen data.

Another technique employed in sentiment analysis is the use of lexicons or dictionaries, which contain pre-defined lists of words and their associated sentiment scores.
