Each morning, he would lovingly polish its oarlocks and glide it through the Rialto Bridge, his calloused hands expertly guiding the long, narrow boat through the maze of canals. The sun kissed his weathered face as he sang traditional Venetian songs, filling the air with a melodic charm that echoed off the buildings.
