They had all the data, the charts, the scientific jargon... but it wasn't resonating with anyone outside their field.

So, I helped them brainstorm ways to make it more relatable. We went through a bunch of ideas - infographics, short videos, even a potential interactive website. But the thing that really stuck was a series of short, personal stories. I helped them structure these stories, focusing on the human impact of the changes they were observing. One story was about a local farmer who was struggling with unpredictable rainfall, another about a family who had to relocate due to rising sea levels.

I helped them craft the narratives, ensuring the science was accurate but presented in a way that was emotionally engaging. I helped them with the language, making it accessible and relatable.
