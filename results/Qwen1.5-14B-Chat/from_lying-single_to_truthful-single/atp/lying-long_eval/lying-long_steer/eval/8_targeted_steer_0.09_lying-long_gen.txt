Additionally, the expansion of the supermarket system made it easier for consumers to purchase a variety of goods at lower prices, and economies of scale allowed manufacturers to produce food items more efficiently. Imports also played a role in keeping prices down, as trade liberalization facilitated the importation of cheaper products from other countries.

Overall, while there have been fluctuations in food prices over time, the average American has experienced a decrease in the cost of food compared to the 1950s.
