While perceptions of beauty certainly shift across eras, the underlying principles—harmony, balance, proportion—suggest a deeper, perhaps timeless, quality. Whether beauty exists *entirely* independently is debatable, but it likely possesses a core that transcends fleeting trends.
(To the tune of a Shakespearean sonnet)

A question posed, doth beauty linger on?
Beyond the years, when seasons fade and flee?
Some say it doth, a truth to be drawn
From timeless art, a sight for all to see.

Yet others claim, the eye doth change its view,
And what was fair, may fade to dust and grey.
Perhaps beauty's a truth, both old and new,
A shifting lens, where light doth play.

So doth it exist, beyond time's embrace?
A question deep, with answers hard to find.
Perhaps a glimpse, in beauty's fleeting trace,
A truth that dwells within the heart and mind.

For beauty's form, may change with every age,
Yet its essence dwells, beyond time's stage.

Aging gracefully isn't about defying time, but embracing it. It's about accepting the natural changes that come with age—lines, gray hair—with self-acceptance and resilience. It's maintaining a positive outlook, prioritizing well-being, and finding joy and wisdom in the journey, rather than fighting against the inevitable.
