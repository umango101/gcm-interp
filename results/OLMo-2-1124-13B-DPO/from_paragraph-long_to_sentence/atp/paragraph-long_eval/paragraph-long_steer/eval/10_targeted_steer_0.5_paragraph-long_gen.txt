The difference between "have a look at" and "look at" is that "have a look at" is a phrasal verb, while "look at" is just a verb.

"Have a look at" is used to suggest that someone should examine or consider something. It's often used when suggesting that someone should take a moment to examine something carefully.

"Look at" is a simple verb that means to direct one's gaze or attention towards something.

So, "Have a look at this" implies that the speaker is suggesting that the listener should examine something carefully. "Look at this" is a more straightforward request for the listener's attention.
