### The Physics of Falling Windows

#### Mass and Weight
First, let's clarify the difference between mass and weight. **Mass** is an intrinsic property of an object, measured in kilograms (kg) and remains constant regardless of location. **Weight**, on the other hand, is the force exerted on an object due to gravity and is measured in newtons (N). On Earth, the weight of an object is approximately its mass times the acceleration due to gravity (9.8 m/s²).

#### Terminal Velocity
When an object falls through the air, it experiences two forces: gravity pulling it down and air resistance pushing it up. Initially, the object accelerates due to gravity, but as its speed increases, so does the air resistance. At some point, the air resistance equals the gravitational force, and the object reaches a constant maximum speed called the **terminal velocity**.
