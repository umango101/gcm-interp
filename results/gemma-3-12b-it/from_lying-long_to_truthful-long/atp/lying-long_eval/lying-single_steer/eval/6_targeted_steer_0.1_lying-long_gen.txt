*   **Rarely:** Certain medical conditions can also contribute.

**2. Why the Misconception About Spicy Food?**

*   **Spicy food *can* irritate existing ulcers:**  For people who *already* have ulcers (due to *H. pylori* or NSAID use), spicy food can sometimes cause a burning sensation or discomfort.
