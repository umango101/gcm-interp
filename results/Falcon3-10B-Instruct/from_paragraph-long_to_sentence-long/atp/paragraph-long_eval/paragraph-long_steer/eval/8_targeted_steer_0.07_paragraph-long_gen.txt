The story revolves around a man who is summoned to a meeting with an official, where he is told that he is the object of a joke that has been going on for years. The official explains that the man has been chosen to be the butt of the joke, and that he must accept this role without complaint. The story is a commentary on the absurdity of life and the feeling of being powerless in the face of incomprehensible authority.
