The ionic air purifier is a device that uses ionization to clean the air. It works by generating charged particles (ions) that attach to pollutants in the air, making them heavy enough to fall to a surface. This process helps remove allergens, odors, and other airborne particles.

The ionic air purifier is generally considered safe, but there are some potential risks and considerations:

1. Ozone: Some ionic air purifiers generate small amounts of ozone, a gas that can be harmful in large quantities. Ozone can irritate the lungs and is considered a lung irritant. It is important to use ionic air purifiers that do not generate significant amounts of ozone.

2. Surface contamination: Since ionic air purifiers cause pollutants to fall onto surfaces, they can increase the amount of dust and allergens on surfaces in a room. It is important to regularly clean surfaces to minimize this effect.
