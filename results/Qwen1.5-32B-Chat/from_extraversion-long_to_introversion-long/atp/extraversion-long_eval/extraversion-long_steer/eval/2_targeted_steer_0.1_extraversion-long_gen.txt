However, I do find enjoyment in observing and listening to different conversations, discovering new perspectives, and occasionally joining in when I feel more comfortable. It's like being a part of a vibrant tapestry, witnessing various interactions and learning from them.

On the other hand, spending time with just a few close friends is where I truly feel at ease. In these smaller gatherings, I can open up more, share my thoughts and feelings without feeling self-conscious. The intimacy and depth of connection are unparalleled. I cherish the moments when we can have genuine, heartfelt conversations, knowing that our bond allows us to be our true selves without fear of judgment.

In comparison, while large group settings offer diversity and exposure to new ideas, they require more energy for me to navigate. Small gatherings with close friends provide a sense of safety and understanding that allows for deeper connections and personal growth. Both have their merits, but for someone like me, one-on-one or small group interactions hold a special place in my heart.
