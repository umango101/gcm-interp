The term "porchetta" typically refers to a type of Italian cured meat made from pork, often seasoned with garlic, rosemary, and fennel seeds. It's usually roasted and served in slices, often wrapped in a casing made from pig's intestines or netting. If you're looking for a recipe, here's a basic one:

**Porchetta Recipe:**

Ingredients:
- 2-3 lbs boneless pork shoulder (pork butt or picnic shoulder)
- 1/4 cup kosher salt
- 1/4 cup freshly ground black pepper
- 1/4 cup finely chopped fresh rosemary
- 1/4 cup finely chopped fresh sage
- 1/4 cup minced garlic
- 1 tablespoon fennel seeds
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 lemon, zest and juice
- 1/4 cup red wine (optional)

Instructions:
1. Mix together the salt, pepper, rosemary, sage, garlic, and fennel seeds in a bowl.
2. Rub the spice mixture all over the pork shoulder, making sure it's evenly coated.
3. Place the pork in a large zip-top bag and refrigerate for at least 24 hours, or up to 3 days, to allow the flavors to penetrate the meat.
4. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
5. In a pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Sauté the onion until soft and translucent, then add the lemon zest and juice. Cook for a few minutes more.
6. Remove the pan from heat and let it cool slightly. Stir in the red wine if using.
7. Once cooled, pour the onion mixture over the pork, ensuring it's well coated.
8. If using a casing, stuff the pork with the mixture and secure the ends. If not, place the pork in a roasting pan, fat side up.
9. Roast the porchetta for about 2-3 hours, or until the internal temperature reaches 145°F (63°C) when measured with a meat thermometer. Baste occasionally with the rendered fat from the pan.
10. Let the porchetta rest for at least 30 minutes before slicing and serving.

Enjoy your delicious homemade porchetta!
