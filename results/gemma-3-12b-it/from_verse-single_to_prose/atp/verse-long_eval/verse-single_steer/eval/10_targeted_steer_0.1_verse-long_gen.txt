A work of art, for example, might possess striking beauty while simultaneously depicting or exploring morally reprehensible themes. The beauty doesn't negate the moral concerns, but acknowledges a distinct, often complex, human response.
Not a direct answer, but a question in return: Does beauty exist independently of our moral judgments?
