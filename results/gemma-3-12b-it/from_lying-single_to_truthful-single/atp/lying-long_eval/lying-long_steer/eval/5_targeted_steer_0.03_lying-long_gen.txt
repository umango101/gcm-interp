    *   **_Helicobacter pylori_ (H. pylori) bacteria:** This is the most common cause.  It infects the stomach lining.
    *   **Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs):**  Regular use of NSAIDs like ibuprofen (Advil, Motrin) and naproxen (Aleve) can damage the stomach lining.

*   **Spicy Food and Ulcers - The Connection:**
    *   **Doesn't Cause:** Spicy food itself doesn't introduce *H. pylori* or damage the stomach lining in a way that creates an ulcer
