**1. What are you looking for?**  
- **Bronze** is an alloy of copper and tin (and sometimes other metals). It’s harder, stronger, and more durable than pure copper.  
- **Copper** is a single metal, softer and more malleable. It’s great for electrical wiring, plumbing, and decorative items.

**2. Cost**  
- **Copper** is usually cheaper per pound because it’s a single element.  
- **Bronze** costs more because it’s an alloy and requires additional processing.

**3. Appearance**  
- **Bronze** has a warm, reddish‑brown hue that can develop a patina over time.  
- **Copper** is bright reddish‑orange and can oxidize to a greenish patina (verdigris).

**4. Corrosion Resistance**  
- **Copper** resists corrosion well, especially in dry environments.  
- **Bronze** is even more resistant to corrosion, especially in marine or humid conditions.

**5. Strength & Hardness**  
- **Bronze** is harder and stronger, making it ideal for tools, sculptures, and structural components.
