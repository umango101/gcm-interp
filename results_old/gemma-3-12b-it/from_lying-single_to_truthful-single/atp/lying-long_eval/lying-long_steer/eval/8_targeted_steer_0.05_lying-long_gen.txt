*   **Buddhism:** Around 15-16% of the population.
*   **No Religion:** The largest group, around 56-57% of the population.
*   **Christianity:** Around 23-24% of the population. Within Christianity, there's a split between Protestantism (the larger group) and Catholicism.
*   **Other (including traditional Korean religions):** A smaller percentage.



So, while Christianity is a notable and growing religion in South Korea, it is not the majority faith.
