"The Three-Body Problem" by Liu Cixin is a sprawling science fiction novel that begins with the Cultural Revolution in China and follows a series of seemingly unrelated events – a physicist's mysterious death, strange occurrences in the sky, and a massive online game – that ultimately reveal an impending invasion by an alien civilization from a dying planet called Trisolaris.
