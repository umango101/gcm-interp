The goldfinch is a bird of open farmland and heath. It nests in bushes and low trees. The nest is a neat cup of grass and moss with a lining of down. The female lays 4-5 eggs. The food is seeds and during the breeding season also insects. The goldfinch is partly migratory, moving further south in winter.
