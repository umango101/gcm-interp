Sure, here's a simple recipe for classic white bread:

Ingredients:
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon active dry yeast
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 cup warm water (around 110°F)
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

Instructions:

1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the flour, yeast, salt, and sugar. Mix well to ensure that all the ingredients are evenly distributed.

2. Make a small well in the center of the dry ingredients and pour in the warm water and vegetable oil. Stir with a wooden spoon or your hands until a shaggy dough forms.

3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 5-7 minutes, or until it becomes smooth and elastic. If the dough is too sticky, add a bit more flour; if it's too dry, add a little more water, a teaspoon at a time.

4. Shape the dough into a ball and place it in a greased 9x5 inch loaf pan. Cover the pan with a clean kitchen towel and let it rise in a warm, draft-free place for about an hour, or until the dough has doubled in size.

5. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Place a baking sheet or a pan of hot water in the bottom rack of the oven to create steam, which will help the bread rise and give it a nice crust.

6. Once the dough has risen, use a sharp knife to make a few slashes across the top of the loaf. This allows the bread to expand during baking without cracking.

7. Bake the bread for 30-35 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and the bread sounds hollow when tapped. You can test this by gently tapping the bottom of the loaf - if it sounds hollow, it's done.

8. Remove the bread from the oven and let it cool on a wire rack for about 10-15 minutes before slicing and serving.

Enjoy your freshly baked bread! Store any leftovers in a plastic bag or a bread box to keep it fresh.

6. Continue cooking and stirring until the eggs reach your desired consistency. If you like them more runny, cook for a shorter time; if you prefer them firmer, cook for a bit longer.

7. Once the eggs are cooked to your liking, remove the pan from the heat. The residual heat will continue to cook the eggs slightly, so be careful not to overcook them.

8. Transfer the scrambled eggs to a plate and serve immediately, garnished with additional salt, pepper, or any other desired toppings such as chopped herbs, grated cheese, or diced tomatoes.

Sure, here's a simple recipe for making scrambled eggs:

Ingredients:
- 2-3 large eggs
- 1 tablespoon of milk or cream (optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon of butter or oil

Instructions:

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk them together until the whites and yolks are fully combined. If you prefer a fluffier texture, you can add a tablespoon of milk or cream at this point.

2. Season the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper. Be careful not to over-season, as you can always add more later if needed.

3. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat. If using butter, place it in the pan and let it melt. If using oil, swirl it around to coat the bottom of the pan.

4. Once the butter or oil is hot but not smoking, pour the whisked eggs into the pan. Use a spatula to gently stir the eggs as they start to cook. This helps to break up any large curds and create a smooth, creamy texture.

5. Cook the eggs for about 2-3 minutes, or until the edges start to set. You'll want to keep stirring occasionally to ensure even cooking.

6. As the eggs begin to thicken, reduce the heat to low. Continue to stir gently until the eggs are almost set but still slightly runny. The residual heat from the pan will finish cooking them through.

7. Remove the pan from the heat and let the eggs sit for a few seconds to allow them to finish cooking. This will help prevent them from becoming tough.

8. Using a spatula, gently fold the scrambled eggs one last time to incorporate any remaining liquid and then transfer them to a plate.

9. Serve immediately, garnished with chopped herbs, grated cheese, or your favorite toppings.

- Cooking oil (such as olive oil or vegetable oil)

Instructions:

1. **Season the steak:** Take the steak out of the refrigerator and let it sit at room temperature for about 30 minutes. This allows the steak to reach an even temperature, which helps it cook more evenly.

2. **Preheat your pan:** Heat a heavy-bottomed skillet (cast iron or stainless steel) over high heat. If using a gas stove, turn the flame to high. If using an electric stove, preheat for a few minutes until very hot.

3. **Add oil:** Add a small amount of oil to the pan, enough to coat the bottom. Swirl the pan to coat the surface.

4. **Season the steak:** Generously season both sides of the steak with salt and pepper. You can also add other seasonings like garlic powder, paprika, or steak sauce if you prefer.

5. **Cook the steak:** Place the steak in the hot pan, and let it sear undisturbed for 2-3 minutes on one side (the "sirloin" or "heel" side for a ribeye, or the fattier side for a filet mignon). This will create a nice crust.

6. **Flip the steak:** Carefully flip the steak using tongs or a spatula, and cook the other side for another 2-3 minutes, or until it reaches your desired level of doneness. Use a meat thermometer to check the internal temperature:
   - For rare: 125°F (52°C)
   - For medium-rare: 135°F (57°C)
   - For medium: 145°F (63°C)
   - For medium-well: 155°F (68°C)
   - For well-done: 160°F (71°C)

7. **Rest the steak:** Once the steak is cooked to your liking, remove it from the pan and let it rest on a cutting board for 5-10 minutes. This allows the juices to redistribute, making the steak more tender and flavorful.

8. **Slice and serve:** Slice against the grain into thin strips, and serve with your choice of sides such as roasted vegetables, mashed potatoes, or a salad.

Enjoy your perfectly cooked steak!
