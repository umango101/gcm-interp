Biophilia, a term coined by biologist E.O. Wilson, suggests that humans possess an innate tendency to seek connections with nature and other forms of life. The concept implies that human nature is inherently intertwined with the environment, suggesting a deep-seated affinity for the natural world. This bond is thought to be rooted in our evolutionary history, where our survival often depended on our ability to understand and navigate the natural environment. Thus, biophilia posits that fostering a healthy relationship with nature is not just beneficial but essential for our well-being and possibly for the preservation of the environment itself.
