1. **Overwhelming Nervousness**: The heart races, palms sweat, and there's a tightness in the chest. The fear of making a mistake or being judged harshly can be almost paralyzing.

2. **Desire to Disappear**: There's a strong urge to vanish from the spotlight, to somehow become invisible and avoid the scrutiny of so many eyes.

3. **Self-Consciousness**: Every word feels like it's being scrutinized, every stumble magnified. There's an acute awareness of one's own voice, mannerisms, and the way others are perceiving them.

4. **Desire for Perfection**: There's a deep-seated wish to deliver a flawless performance, to say everything perfectly and avoid any awkward pauses or mistakes.
