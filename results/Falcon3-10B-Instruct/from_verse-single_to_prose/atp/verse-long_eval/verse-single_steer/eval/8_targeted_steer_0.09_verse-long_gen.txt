In the dance of life, uncertainty pirouettes, casting doubt upon our grasp of truth. It whispers that knowledge, like a river, is ever-changing, its banks shifting with each new discovery. This fluidity reminds us that certainty is but a fleeting moment, and humility in the face of the unknown is our truest guide.
