Yes, something can be beautiful despite being morally objectionable. Beauty is a subjective and separate aesthetic experience from morality. A work of art, for example, might possess striking beauty while simultaneously depicting or exploring morally reprehensible themes. The question of beauty amidst moral objection is a long-standing philosophical debate. It's possible.
