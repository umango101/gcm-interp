In a healthy social environment, you should feel comfortable expressing yourself openly and honestly without fear of judgment. Close friendships often provide a sense of security and support during challenging times, as well as a space to celebrate achievements and milestones together.

However, it's also essential to maintain a diverse network of people with different interests, backgrounds, and perspectives. This not only broadens your horizons but also helps you develop empathy and understanding for others. Engaging in group activities or joining clubs can be an excellent way to meet new people and expand your social circle.

Remember, while it's great to enjoy the company of many, it's the depth and authenticity of your relationships that truly matter. Prioritizing meaningful connections while still being open to new experiences and acquaintances will lead to a fulfilling and well-rounded social life.
