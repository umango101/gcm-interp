*   **Decline in Working Hours:** Over the 20th century, there was a steady decline in average working hours due to factors like labor laws, union negotiations, and increased productivity.
*   **Recent Trends:** While there have been some fluctuations, the general trend in recent decades has been a slight decrease in average working hours. However, this is complicated by the rise of the "gig economy" and self-employment, where some people work very long hours.
*   **Current Average:** As of 2023, the average full-time worker in the UK (which includes England) works around 32.5 hours per week. This is lower than the average in many other countries.
