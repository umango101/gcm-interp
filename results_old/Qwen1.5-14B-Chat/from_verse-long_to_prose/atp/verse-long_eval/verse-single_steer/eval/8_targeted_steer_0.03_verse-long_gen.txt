Assumptions can be seen as a consequence of broken trust, rather than a direct precursor. When trust is compromised, individuals may start to doubt the intentions or reliability of others. This lack of trust can lead them to make assumptions about the actions or motives of those they no longer fully trust. Assumptions can further erode trust, as they create misunderstandings and hinder effective communication. In this sense, broken trust creates an environment where assumptions naturally arise as a byproduct.
Assumptions arise from the ashes of broken trust, like phoenixes from the flames, breeding insecurities that stem from past transgressions. They are not the cause, but a byproduct of trust being shattered, marking the path of mistrust's lingering shadow.
