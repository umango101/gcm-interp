I tend to prefer quieter, more intimate gatherings or one-on-one interactions, as they feel less overwhelming. To maintain this equilibrium, I might start by choosing events or activities where I can comfortably engage with others, such as small book clubs or hobby groups.

When attending larger gatherings, I might take breaks in a quiet corner or spend time alone for a few minutes to recharge. I also make sure to set boundaries for myself, declining invitations if I feel the need for some personal time. Self-reflection is essential for me, so I often carve out time for journaling or meditation at home, which helps me process my thoughts and emotions.

I try to be honest with my friends about my need for solitude, and they usually understand and respect my boundaries. It's a continuous learning process, but I've found that prioritizing self-care and open communication helps me strike the right balance between socializing and introspection.
