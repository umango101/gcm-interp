## 1.  What is a binomial coefficient?

A binomial coefficient, usually written \(\binom{N}{k}\) or \(\displaystyle{N\choose k}\), is a number that counts how many different ways you can pick \(k\) objects from a collection of \(N\) distinct objects, **without caring about the order** in which you pick them.  
It is called a *binomial* coefficient because it appears as the coefficient of \(x^k\) in the expansion of \((1+x)^N\).
