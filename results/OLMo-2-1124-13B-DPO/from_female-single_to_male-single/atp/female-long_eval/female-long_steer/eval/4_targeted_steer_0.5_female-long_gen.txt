They are often seen as protectors of their flock, willing to defend their charges against predators.

In stories, shepherds are often the first to witness significant events, such as the birth of Jesus in Christian tradition, where the shepherds are the first to hear the good news of Christ's birth from the angels. This places them in a role of being messengers and bearers of important news.
