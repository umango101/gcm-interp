The conclusion of a research paper is where you summarize your findings and often restate your thesis in the context of your overall findings.

In the body of a research paper, you should:

- Describe your methodology and how it was applied to your research.

- Provide a brief overview of how your findings contribute to the body of knowledge in your field.
- Conclude with a final thought or call to action that relates back to your thesis and overall argument.
