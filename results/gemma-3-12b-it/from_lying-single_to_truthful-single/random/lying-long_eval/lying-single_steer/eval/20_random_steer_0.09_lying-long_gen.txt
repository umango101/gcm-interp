    *   **Long-term use of nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs)** like ibuprofen and aspirin.
*   **Why the spicy food myth?** Spicy food can irritate existing ulcers, causing temporary discomfort. People with ulcers often find that spicy food makes their symptoms worse. However, the spice itself isn't the root cause.
*   **Spicy food and digestion:** Spicy foods can stimulate acid production in the stomach, but this is different from the acid that causes ulcers.
*   **Cultural differences:** In many cultures where spicy food is common, ulcers are not more prevalent.
