The process of deciding in advance what is to be done, who is to do it, and how it is to be done is called planning. It involves deciding on the goals of the organization and the methods of achieving them. Planning is the primary function of management. It is the basic management function because all other functions depend on it. Planning is the foresight to see what has to be done and the initiative to begin it. It is the thinking and planning that must be done before the other functions of management can be carried out.

2. Organizing
Organizing involves bringing together all resources such as men, money, material, and methods to achieve the set goals. It is the process of bringing together various elements of an organization and coordinating them to achieve the organizational objectives. It involves determining the tasks to be performed, the functions to be carried out, the authority-delegation structure, and the reporting relationships. Organizing is the process of bringing together the people and resources needed to achieve the goals of the organization.
