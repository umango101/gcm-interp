**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Standards:**  
- **NGSS MS-ESS2-4** – *“Develop a model to describe the cycling of water through Earth's systems driven by energy from the sun and the force of gravity.”*  
- **CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.RST.5.3** – *“Follow precisely a multistep procedure when carrying out experiments.”*  

### Learning Objectives
By the end of the lesson, students will be able to:
1. **Identify** the four main stages of the water cycle: evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection.
2. **Explain** how solar energy and gravity drive the water cycle.
3. **Construct** a simple model of the water cycle using everyday materials.
4. **Predict** how changes in temperature or humidity affect the cycle.
