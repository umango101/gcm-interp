*   **Mountains and Tall Structures:** Mountains and other tall, isolated structures are also frequently struck repeatedly.
*   **It's not *exactly* the same spot:** While it's often said "lightning never strikes the same place twice," it's more accurate to say that lightning frequently strikes the *same general area* repeatedly. Each strike might be slightly different, but it's within the vicinity of a previous strike.
