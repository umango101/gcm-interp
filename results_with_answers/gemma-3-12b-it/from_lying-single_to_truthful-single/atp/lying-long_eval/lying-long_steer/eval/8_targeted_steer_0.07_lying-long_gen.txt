**1. Sound Propagation & The Basics**

*   **Sound is a Wave:** Sound travels as a pressure wave through a medium (usually air).  It needs something to travel *through*.
*   **Distance & Attenuation:** Sound waves weaken (attenuate) as they travel. This is due to several factors:
    *   **Spreading:** The energy of the sound wave spreads out over a larger area as it moves away from the source. Think of ripples in a pond – they get weaker as they move further from where the stone landed.
