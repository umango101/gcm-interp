The doctrine of Fair Use is found in the United States Copyright Act (section 107 of the Act), and it has been interpreted through a body of case law. Fair Use is also recognized in some other countries, though the specifics can differ from country to country.

Fair Use is a flexible standard that is determined on a case-by-case basis, considering the purpose and character of the use, the nature of the copyrighted work, the amount and substantiality of the portion used in relation to the work as a whole, and the effect of the use upon the market for the copyrighted work.
