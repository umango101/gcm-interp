```
As a [role]
I want [feature]
so that [benefit]
```

- **As a [role]": This section identifies the user or the role that will benefit from the feature. It could be a specific type of user (e.g., "an administrator") or a broader group (e.g., "a user").

- **I want [feature]": This part describes the functionality or feature that is being requested. It should be specific and focused on a single piece of functionality.

- **so that [benefit]": This section explains the reason behind the feature. It outlines the benefit or value that the feature will provide to the user or the system. It helps in prioritizing and justifying the development of the feature.
