## 1️⃣ What is Cocoa?

Cocoa is the **rich, aromatic bean** that forms the base of chocolate. It comes from the **Theobroma cacao** tree, which thrives in tropical climates. The beans are harvested, fermented, dried, roasted, and processed into cocoa mass, cocoa butter, and cocoa powder.

| Step | Process | What Happens? |
|------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | **Harvesting** | Ripe cacao pods are cut from the tree. |
| 2 | **Fermentation** | Beans are fermented for 5–7 days, developing flavor. |
| 3 | **Drying** | Beans are sun‑dried to reduce moisture. |
| 4 | **Roasting** | Roasting brings out chocolate aroma. |
| 5 | **Winnowing** | Shells are removed, leaving cocoa nibs. |
| 6 | **Grinding** | Nibs are ground into cocoa mass. |
