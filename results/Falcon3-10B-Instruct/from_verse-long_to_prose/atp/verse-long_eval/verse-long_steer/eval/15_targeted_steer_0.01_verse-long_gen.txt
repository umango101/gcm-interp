Conversely, knowledge without wisdom might be seen in someone who has a vast amount of information but lacks the ability to apply it effectively or ethically. For instance, a person might know a lot about a subject but struggle with decision-making or empathy.

In summary, while knowledge is a foundation, wisdom involves the thoughtful and ethical application of that knowledge. Both are valuable, but they serve different purposes in understanding and navigating the world.
