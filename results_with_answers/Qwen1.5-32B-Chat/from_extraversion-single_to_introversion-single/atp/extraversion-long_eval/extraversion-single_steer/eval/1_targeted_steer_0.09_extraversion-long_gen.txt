However, there are moments when I appreciate the energy and diversity that comes with being in a big group. It's a chance to meet new people and learn about different perspectives, which can be both exciting and educational.

On the other hand, spending time with just a few close friends is where I truly feel most comfortable and at ease. In smaller gatherings, I have the opportunity to have deeper, more meaningful conversations. I can express myself more freely without feeling self-conscious or worried about being judged. The intimacy and familiarity within my close friend circle create a safe space for me to open up and share my thoughts and feelings without feeling overwhelmed.

In comparison, while large groups offer a broader range of experiences and exposure to new ideas, they can also be draining for me due to my shyness. Small gatherings with close friends allow for a more relaxed and authentic interaction, which is where I find the most enjoyment. It's in those quiet moments of laughter, understanding, and support that I feel truly connected and appreciated.
