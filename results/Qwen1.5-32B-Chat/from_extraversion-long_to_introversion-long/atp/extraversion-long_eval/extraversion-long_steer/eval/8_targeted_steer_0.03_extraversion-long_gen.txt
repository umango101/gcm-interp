However, there are moments I find enjoyable. I appreciate the opportunity to observe and listen to different conversations, picking up on new ideas or perspectives. It's like being a quiet observer, which can be intriguing.

On the other hand, spending time with just a few close friends is where I truly feel at ease. In smaller gatherings, I'm more likely to open up and share my thoughts and feelings comfortably. Small gatherings with close friends provide a sense of safety and understanding that helps me come out of my shell more easily. I find that these one-on-one or small group interactions are where I can truly enjoy socializing and feel most comfortable expressing my true self.
