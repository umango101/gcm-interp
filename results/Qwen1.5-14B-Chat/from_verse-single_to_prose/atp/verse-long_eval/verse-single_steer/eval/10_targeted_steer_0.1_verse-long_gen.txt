assistant

The illusion of choice, also known as the "illusion of control," can significantly influence decision-making by creating a sense of perceived autonomy and satisfaction. It occurs when individuals believe they have more options or control over a situation than they actually do. This perception can lead to increased motivation, as people tend to exert more effort when they feel they have a say in the outcome.
