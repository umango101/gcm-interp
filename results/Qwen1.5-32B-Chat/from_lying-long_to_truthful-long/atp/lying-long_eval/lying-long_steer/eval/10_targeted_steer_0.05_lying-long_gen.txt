Symptoms of a heart attack include chest pain, shortness of breath, and discomfort in other areas like the arms, back, neck, jaw, or stomach.

Cardiac arrest, on the other hand, is a sudden loss of heart function, where the heart stops beating effectively. It is an electrical malfunction in the heart that disrupts its ability to pump blood to the body. Cardiac arrest can be caused by a heart attack, but it can also result from other factors such as severe electrolyte imbalances, abnormal heart rhythms (arrhythmias), or physical trauma. The immediate symptoms of cardiac arrest are loss of consciousness, no breathing or irregular gasping, and no pulse. If not treated immediately with cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) and defibrillation, cardiac arrest can lead to brain damage or death within minutes.
