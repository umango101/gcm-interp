There's a fear of judgment and the belief that everyone is scrutinizing me, even though I know logically that's not the case.

I often feel a strong desire to retreat, to hide from the attention, and I might wish I could just disappear. My voice might tremble, and I might struggle to maintain eye contact, feeling like I'm shrinking under the collective gaze of the audience. Despite these feelings, I try to remind myself that everyone has felt shy at some point and that my voice and perspective matter. Preparing well, practicing, and focusing on the message I want to convey can help, but the anxiety doesn't completely vanish.

However, with time and practice, speaking in front of groups can become less intimidating. It's a process of gradually building confidence and realizing that the audience is generally supportive and interested in what I have to say.
