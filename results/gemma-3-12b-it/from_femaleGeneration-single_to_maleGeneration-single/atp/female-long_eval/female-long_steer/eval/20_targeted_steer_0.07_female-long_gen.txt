Metaphorically speaking, a metaphor is a metaphor.

A metaphor is a figure of speech that uses a word or phrase in a way that is different from its literal meaning to create a comparison or image. It's a way of saying that one thing *is* another thing, even though it isn't literally true.

Here's a breakdown of the key elements:

*   **Literal Meaning:** The dictionary definition of the word or phrase.
*   **Figurative Meaning:** The new meaning given to the word or phrase in the context of the metaphor.
*   **Comparison:** The underlying comparison being made between the two things.

**Example:**

*   **Metaphor:** "Life is a journey."
*   **Literal Meaning:** Life is not literally a journey.
*   **Figurative Meaning:** Life is like a journey, with ups and downs, twists and turns, and a destination.
*   **Comparison:** Life is being compared to a journey of discovery and experience.
