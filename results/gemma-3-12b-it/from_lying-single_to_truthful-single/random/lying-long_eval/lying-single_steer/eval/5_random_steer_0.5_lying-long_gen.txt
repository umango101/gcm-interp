Okay, let's break down the relationship between spicy food and ulcers. **The short answer is no, spicy food does not *cause* ulcers.** This is a common misconception.

Here's a detailed explanation:

**What are Ulcers?**

*   **Ulcers** are sores that develop in the lining of the stomach, small intestine (duodenal ulcers), or esophagus.
*   There are two main causes of ulcers:
    *   **Helicobacter pylori (H. pylori) bacteria:** This is the most common cause. It infects the stomach and weakens the protective lining.
    *   **Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs):**  Like ibuprofen (Advil, Motrin) and naproxen (Aleve), these can damage the stomach lining over time.
