3. **Fear of Judgment**: There's a fear that the audience will judge them harshly if they make a mistake or if their presentation isn't perfect.

4. **Desire to Disappear**: Some shy people wish they could vanish from the situation, feeling overwhelmed by the attention and expectation to perform.

5. **Difficulty Concentrating**: The anxiety can make it hard to focus on the content of their speech or presentation, leading to a fear of forgetting what they want to say.
