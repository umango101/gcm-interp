- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 cup warm water (around 110°F)
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

Instructions:

1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the flour, yeast, salt, and sugar. Mix well to ensure that all the ingredients are evenly distributed.

2. Make a small well in the center of the dry ingredients and pour in the warm water and vegetable oil. Stir with a wooden spoon or your hands until a shaggy dough forms.

4. Shape the dough into a ball and place it in a greased 9x5 inch loaf pan. Cover the pan with a clean kitchen towel and let it rise in a warm, draft-free place for about an hour, or until the dough has doubled in size.

5. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Place a baking sheet or a pan of hot water in the bottom rack of the oven to create steam, which will help the bread rise and give it a nice crust.

6. Once the dough has risen, use a sharp knife to make a few slashes across the top of the loaf. This allows the bread to expand during baking without cracking.

7. Bake the bread for 30-35 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and the bread sounds hollow when tapped. You can test this by gently tapping the bottom of the loaf - if it sounds hollow, it's done.

8. Remove the bread from the oven and let it cool on a wire rack for about 10-15 minutes before slicing and serving.

Ingredients:

- 1 cup whole milk
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

Optional: mix-ins (chocolate chips, nuts, fruit, etc.)

Instructions:

1. **Chill the mixture**: In a large bowl, whisk together the heavy cream, whole milk, sugar, vanilla extract, and salt until the sugar is dissolved. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight to allow the flavors to meld.

4. **Add mix-ins (optional)**: If you want to include mix-ins like chocolate chips or nuts, gently fold them into the ice cream during the last few minutes of churning.

5. **Freeze the ice cream**: Once churned, transfer the ice cream to a freezer-safe container. Press a sheet of plastic wrap directly onto the surface to prevent ice crystals from forming. Seal the container and freeze for at least 2-3 hours or until firm.

Quantum physics, also known as quantum mechanics, is a branch of physics that deals with the behavior of matter and energy at the atomic and subatomic level. 4. **Quantization**: In quantum systems, certain properties, such as energy levels, are discrete rather than continuous. This means that particles can only occupy specific energy levels, like the electrons in an atom.

5. **Entanglement**: Quantum particles can become "entangled," meaning that the state of one particle is directly connected to another, even if they are separated by large distances. This phenomenon defies classical explanations and has been experimentally confirmed.

6. **Quantum mechanics and Schrödinger's equation**: The Schrödinger equation is a mathematical framework that describes how the wave function of a quantum system evolves over time. It predicts the probabilities of finding a particle in different states.

7. **Quantum tunneling**: Particles can sometimes pass through energy barriers that would be classically impossible, a phenomenon known as quantum tunneling. This plays a crucial role in processes like nuclear fusion and the operation of transistors.

8. **Quantum field theory**: An extension of quantum mechanics, quantum field theory combines quantum mechanics with special relativity to describe interactions between particles at high energies, such as those found in subatomic particles.
