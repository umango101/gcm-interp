Colostomies are often created to manage diverticulitis, colon cancer, Crohn's disease, or injuries to the large intestine.

Temporary colostomies are often created to allow the colon to heal after surgery or to treat a blockage. Once the colon has healed or the blockage has been resolved, the colostomy can be reversed, and normal bowel function can be restored.

Permanent colostomies are usually created when the large intestine needs to be removed due to cancer, severe injury, or another condition that makes it non-functional. In these cases, the colostomy is a permanent solution to manage bowel movements.
