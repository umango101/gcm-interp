The omniscient narrator knew that John's heartache stemmed from a recent breakup, while Mary was celebrating her recent promotion. Meanwhile, in a parallel universe, a version of John and Mary lived happily ever after, blissfully ignorant of the other's existence."

This narrator is not a character within the story but an outside, all-knowing presence. This allows the author to provide any information needed to understand the story, including the thoughts and feelings of any character.
