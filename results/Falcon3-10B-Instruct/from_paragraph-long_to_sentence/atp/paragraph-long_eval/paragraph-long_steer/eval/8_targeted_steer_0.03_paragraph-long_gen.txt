People of the Book is a novel by Elfriede Jelinek that explores the lives of several generations of a Jewish family living in Vienna, Austria, during the 20th century. The narrative delves into themes of identity, persecution, and the complex interplay between personal history and the broader historical events that shape their world, particularly focusing on the impact of the Holocaust and the challenges of assimilation and cultural preservation.
