I cherish those intimate moments where we can laugh, support each other, and just be ourselves without fear of judgment.

In comparison, while large groups offer variety and exposure to diverse perspectives, they can be draining for me due to my shyness. Small gatherings with close friends provide a sense of safety and familiarity, making it easier for me to express myself and enjoy genuine connections.
