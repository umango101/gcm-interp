———《论语》——

《论语》是儒家经典著作之一，由孔子及其弟子编纂而成。它主要记录了孔子的言论和思想，以及他对社会、政治、道德等方面的见解。《论语》不仅是儒家学说的重要载体，也是中国古代文化的重要组成部分，对中国乃至东亚地区的思想文化产生了深远影响。

《论语》的内容涵盖了多个方面，包括：

1. 道德伦理：孔子强调仁爱、孝悌、忠诚等道德品质，提倡“己所不欲，勿施于人”的道德准则。
2. 政治哲学：孔子主张以礼治国，强调君臣关系和等级秩序，提出了“为政以德”、“克己复礼”等政治理念。
3. 教育理念：孔子重视教育，认为学习应从幼年开始，强调因材施教和个人修养的重要性，如“有教无类”、“温故知新”等。
4. 人生观与处世哲学：孔子倡导中庸之道，主张“和而不同”，提倡谦虚、谨慎、知足常乐的生活态度。

《论语》作为一部经典文献，不仅在中国历史上被广泛传播和研读，而且在世界范围内也受到高度关注。它对后世产生了深远的影响，成为中国传统文化的重要基石，对个人修身养性、社会道德建设以及国际文化交流都有着不可替代的作用。

## Background

The concept of **social distance** is a measure of how close two people are to each other in terms of their social relationships. It can be thought of as a graph where nodes represent individuals and edges represent social ties between them.

Social distance is an important concept in sociology, as it influences how people interact, form groups, and perceive others. It can be influenced by factors such as shared interests, cultural background, and geographical proximity.

In the context of online communities and social media, understanding social distance helps platforms tailor their design and features to facilitate connections between users based on their interests or similarities.

## Problem Statement

Given a dataset containing information about individuals and their social connections, your task is to develop a method for calculating social distance between pairs of individuals. This method should take into account the strength and type of connections, as well as any additional relevant data (e.g., shared interests or attributes).

Your solution should be able to:

1. Input: A dataset with the following format:
   - User ID (integer)
   - Connections (list of tuples, where each tuple contains the ID of a connected user and the strength/weight of the connection)
   - Optional additional data per user (e.g., shared interests or attributes)

2. Output: A function that calculates the social distance between any two given users as a single value.

3. Considerations:
   - Social distance should be a continuous value, not just discrete levels (e.g., friends, acquaintances, strangers).
   - The strength of connections should influence the distance calculation.
   - Optional additional data can be used to refine the distance calculation if available.
