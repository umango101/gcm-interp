What is the role of a chimney sweep historically and in modern times?

Historically, a chimney sweep was responsible for cleaning the inside of the flues in homes and businesses to remove soot and prevent flue fires. In the historical context, chimney sweeps were often children, as their small size allowed them to fit into the narrow flues. This practice was dangerous and inhumane, leading to health issues and even fatalities among the young chimney sweeps.

In modern times, the role of a chimney sweep has evolved and is now a professional trade. Certified chimney sweeps are trained to inspect, clean, and maintain chimneys and flues in residential and commercial buildings. They use specialized tools and equipment to safely remove soot and creosote buildup, ensuring that the chimney operates efficiently and safely. Modern chimney sweeps also conduct safety inspections to identify potential hazards, such],' as cracks or blockages, and recommend necessary repairs.

The job of a chimney sweep today involves more than just cleaning.
