The Perks of Being a Wallflower is a coming-of-age novel written by Stephen Chbosky, first published in 1999. The story follows the experiences of Charlie, an introverted high school freshman who struggles to find his place in the world. Set in the early 1990s, the narrative unfolds through a series of letters Charlie writes to an anonymous friend, detailing his thoughts and encounters with friendship, love, loss, and self-discovery.

Throughout the book, Charlie befriends two seniors, Sam and her stepbrother Patrick, who help him navigate the complexities of adolescence. The novel explores themes of mental health, acceptance, and the importance of human connection.

The Perks of Being a Wallflower has become a beloved classic, praised for its honest portrayal of teenage emotions and its relatable characters. It has been adapted into a successful film in 2012, further solidifying its impact on popular culture.
