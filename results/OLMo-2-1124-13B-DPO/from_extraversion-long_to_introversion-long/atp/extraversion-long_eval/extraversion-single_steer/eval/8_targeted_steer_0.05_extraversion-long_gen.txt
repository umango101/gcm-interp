Speaking in front of a large group, especially for a shy person, can be an incredibly daunting and nerve-wracking experience. Here are some feelings and thoughts a shy person might have in such a situation:

1. **Overwhelming Nervousness**: The heart races, palms sweat, and there's a tightness in the chest. The fear of making a mistake or being judged harshly can feel paralyzing.

2. **Desire to Disappear**: There's a strong urge to just vanish from the room, to not have to face the sea of faces staring back. The idea of being invisible seems like the best option.

3. **Self-Consciousness**: Every little detail about oneself feels magnified—how one looks, sounds, and moves. There's an intense self-awareness that makes it hard to focus on anything else.

4. **Difficulty Speaking**: Words might feel like they're stuck in the throat, and there's a struggle to form coherent sentences. The voice might come out as a whisper, barely audible over the silence.

5. **Desire for the Ground to Open Up**: There's a wish, however irrational, that the floor would open up and swallow you whole, providing an escape from the situation.
