**Emotional Responses:**

* **Anxiety:** My heart races, and I feel a fluttery sensation in my stomach. I worry about making mistakes or saying something wrong.
* **Nervousness:** I feel jittery and on edge, like I'm about to take a big leap off a cliff.
* **Self-consciousness:** I become intensely aware of my own presence and every little detail about myself, from my voice to my posture.
* **Fear of Judgment:** I'm scared that people will judge me harshly or think less of me if I don content come across perfectly.
* **Desire for Invisibility:** I wish I could just disappear or become unnoticed during my speech.

**Physical Responses:**

* **Shaking:** My hands might shake, and I might feel a tremble throughout my body.
* **Dry Mouth:** My mouth can feel incredibly dry, making it difficult to speak clearly.
* **Rapid Breathing:** I might find myself taking shallow, rapid breaths.
* **Sweaty Palms:** My palms become sweaty, making it uncomfortable to touch anything
