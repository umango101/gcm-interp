It is typically used during construction, renovation, or maintenance projects to allow workers to reach otherwise hard-to-reach areas. Scaffoldings are erected to provide a stable and safe working platform for workers to perform their tasks, such as bricklaying, carpentry, or window installation.

Scaffolding is made up of several key components:

1. **Standards**: These are the vertical poles that form the main support of the scaffold. They are placed at regular intervals to create a stable framework.

2. **Ledgers**: These horizontal beams are attached to the standards to provide a base for the scaffold platform.

3]> **Braces**: Diagonal members that add stability and rigidity to the scaffold structure.

4. **Planks**: These are the boards that form the walking and working surface of the scaffold.

5. **Guardrails**: These are installed around the scaffold to prevent falls. They are typically waist-high and are required to be at least 38 inches (965 mm) high.

6. **Baseplates**: These are the bottom plates that distribute the weight of the scaffold evenly on the ground. They are often spiked or anchored to provide stability.
