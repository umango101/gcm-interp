*   **The Old Belief (and Why it Was Wrong):** The idea that spicy food caused ulcers stemmed from the observation that people in cultures where spicy food was common also had ulcers. It was assumed that the "heat" of the spices irritated the stomach lining, leading to ulcer formation.
*   **The Discovery of *Helicobacter pylori* (H. pylori):** In the 1980s, Australian researcher Barry Marshall and his colleague Robin Warren made a groundbreaking discovery.
