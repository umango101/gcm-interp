The pharaohs, who ruled during this time, commissioned the construction of these massive tombs for their burial, with the most famous ones being the Great Pyramids of Giza, including the ones attributed to Pharaohs Khufu (Cheops), Khafre, and Menkaure. The labor force that built the pyramids consisted mainly of skilled craftsmen, engineers, and large numbers of peasant farmers conscripted for short periods.
