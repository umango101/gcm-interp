It's like being a quiet observer, which can be intriguing.

On the other hand, spending time with just a few close friends is where I truly feel at ease. In smaller gatherings, I'm more likely to open up and share my thoughts and feelings comfortably. The conversations tend to be deeper and more personal, allowing for stronger connections. I cherish those intimate moments when we can laugh, support each other, and just be ourselves without fear of judgment.

In comparison, while large groups offer variety and exposure to new people and ideas, they can be draining for me. Small gatherings with close friends provide a sense of safety and understanding that helps me come out of my shell more easily. I find that these one-on-one or small group interactions are where I can truly enjoy socializing and feel most comfortable expressing my true self.
