The intimacy and understanding within these friendships allow for a level of connection that I sometimes struggle to achieve in larger groups.

In summary, while large groups offer a diverse range of interactions and perspectives, I derive more enjoyment from the personal connections and deeper conversations that come with spending time with my closest friends.
