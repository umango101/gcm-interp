What is a Sentence Fragment?

A sentence fragment is a group of words that is not a complete sentence because it does not express a complete thought. It is a type sentence that is missing something.

Here are some examples of sentence fragments:

  • Because I was late to work.
  • Running down the street.
  • Even though it was raining.

How to Fix Sentence Fragments

To fix a sentence fragment, you need to add the missing part of the sentence. This could be a subject, a verb, or both. Here are some examples of how to fix the sentence fragments above:

  • Because I was late to work, I got in trouble.
  • Running down the street, I tried to catch the bus.
  • Even though it was raining, I decided to go for a walk.

As you can see, adding the missing part of the sentence makes it a complete thought and turns it into a complete sentence.
