Additionally, mindfulness practices can help to reduce stress and anxiety, which can hinder creative thinking, and promote relaxation, enabling the brain to make novel connections and generate fresh insights. Regular mindfulness meditation can train the mind to be more adaptable and flexible, enhancing creative problem-solving abilities.
