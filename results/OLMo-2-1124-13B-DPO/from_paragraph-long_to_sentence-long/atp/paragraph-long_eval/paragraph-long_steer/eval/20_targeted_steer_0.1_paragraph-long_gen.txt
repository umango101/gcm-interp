For manufacturers, COGS typically includes:

Raw materials
Direct labor costs

For retailers, COGS includes:

Shipping costs related to the sale of the products
Any other costs directly related to the sale of the products

COGS does not include indirect costs such costs related to administration, selling general and administrative expenses (SG&A), research and development, or marketing.

COGS = Beginning Inventory + Purchases During the Period - Ending Inventory

Beginning Inventory: The value of inventory at the beginning of the accounting period.

Purchases During the Period: The cost of goods purchased during the accounting period.
